# AA app mark

The master is saved in `public/app-icons/brand-master.png`. It was generated with the built-in image generation tool. `desktop/build-icons.mjs` creates the packaged web, phone and desktop icon sizes from that master.

## Generation prompt

Create a finished premium app icon for Arpit Agarwala's personal productivity and tools app. One square icon only, full bleed 1024 by 1024. Deep near-black graphite background #111315, flat warm ivory geometric monogram of two interlocking capital A letters, carefully balanced negative space, sharp architectural geometry with restrained softly rounded joins. Quiet professional visual identity, confident and understated, legible at 24 pixels. Monogram centered within central 64% of canvas with generous uniform safe margins for app icon masking. No text other than the abstract AA monogram. No border, no bevel, no shadow, no gradients, no glass, no mockup, no decorative objects. Tiny restrained muted blue-grey accent is optional only if it improves the monogram. Deliver a single polished flat graphic suitable for Android, iPhone, desktop and web app icons.
